# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Psychotherapists and their practices — from solo practitioners to small teams with multiple staff (clinicians, front desk, billing admin, practice admin roles). Primary job: running the day-to-day business of a talk-therapy practice — scheduling, clinical documentation, billing, and client communication — without fighting complicated, dated practice-management software.

## Product Purpose

Willow is practice-management software for therapy practices: scheduling and telehealth, session notes, consent/intake forms, invoicing with real online payment collection, secure client messaging, and a client-facing portal for self-service booking and messages. It replaces the spreadsheet/paper/legacy-EHR patchwork many practices run on today.

## Positioning

Simplicity and design quality, not a longer feature list. The category (SimplePractice, Owl Practice, Jane App, TheraNest, etc.) is dominated by software that feels like enterprise EHR — cluttered, dated interfaces, steep learning curves. Willow is deliberately minimal and considered — an uncluttered, Apple-inspired interface — while still covering a practice's real day-to-day needs (billing, telehealth, client portal, multi-staff roles). The pitch is "it just works, and it's actually pleasant to use," not "we have the most features."

Practice size is not the pitch. Willow serves solo practitioners and small multi-staff practices alike — multi-staff support (role-based access: Practice Admin, Clinician, Front Desk, Billing Admin; staff invites; offboarding with client reassignment) is real and built today, not aspirational. Do not position the product as solo-only.

## Operating Context

Practices manage: client records, appointment scheduling (in-person and telehealth via Daily.co), session notes (SOAP format), consent/intake forms, invoicing and online payments (via each practice's own connected Stripe account), secure messaging with clients (plus inbound email-reply routing), and staff management. Clients get their own portal to book appointments, message their practitioner, sign forms, and pay invoices online.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Real, working Stripe billing ($49/month CAD subscription) and Stripe Connect (each practice connects its own account so clients pay invoices directly to that practice — Willow never holds the funds).
- Multi-staff support is real and built. A dedicated group-practice oversight/analytics dashboard is not built yet (on hold).
- AI-assisted note-taking and insurance claim submission/tracking are not built.
- Mobile-responsive throughout, on both the staff app and the client portal.
- Deployed at willow.sulosystems.com; the company behind it is Sulo Systems Inc. (Ontario, Canada).

## Brand Commitments

Name: Willow. Visual identity: a teal → blue → purple → coral gradient mark with a white location-pin glyph (already the app's sidebar logo, browser favicon, and sign-in page mark). The existing product UI uses a light, minimal, Apple-inspired design language — soft rounded corners, restrained color, generous whitespace, a token-based color system. The landing page must feel like it belongs to the same product, not a separate marketing aesthetic bolted on top.

## Evidence on Hand

No public customer testimonials, logos, or case studies are confirmed for use — do not fabricate or imply any (no invented reviews, customer counts, or "trusted by" claims). Pricing is real and confirmed for display: $49/month CAD. No press, awards, or usage-stat proof exists yet.

## Product Principles

1. Design quality is a feature, not decoration — the landing page must actually demonstrate the calm, uncluttered interface it's selling, not just claim it in copy.
2. Practice size is not the pitch — serves solo practitioners and small multi-staff practices alike; never position as solo-only.
3. Every claim on the page must be true today — no aspirational or invented features, proof, or metrics.
4. The page exists to convert a visiting therapist into a signup, with a persistent, obvious path to `/login` and `/signup`.
